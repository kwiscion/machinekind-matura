## Method (`harness/contamination_check.py`, stdlib only)

**Units compared:**

- 40 **prompt** units: each full runner prompt, normalized.
- 59 **excerpt** units: every distinct blank-line paragraph of the prompts with ≥ 100 normalized chars. These cover the arkusz source-pack excerpts and question stems. Shared group preambles are deduplicated.

**Normalization:** NFKC, lowercase, answer placeholders removed, punctuation turned into spaces, whitespace collapsed. The same normalization is applied to every chunk text.

**Signals, per unit against all 3481 chunks:**

1. **Exact:** the unit's SHA-256 equals a chunk's SHA-256, or either text is a substring of the other (chunks ≥ 40 chars).
2. **Char n-grams (8 and 13):**
   - Jaccard;
   - containment |U∩C|/|U| for the best chunk;
   - corpus-wide 13-gram containment (the share of the unit's 13-grams found in any chunk);
   - longest common normalized substring with the best-containment chunk.
3. **BM25:** the top 5 chunks from Bukareszt's own `BM25Index` (k1 1.5, b 0.75, title weight 1.0), queried with each unit. For each unit I report the best 13-gram containment among those top 5.

**Thresholds:**

- **Flag:** any exact or substring hit, or 13-gram containment ≥ 0.20. Both were fixed before the first run. The LCS ≥ 80 chars flag was added after the first run as a second, stricter signal.
- **Review:** 13-gram containment ≥ 0.08.

**Positive control** (synthetic prompts in the scratchpad, not committed): I planted 150–600 char slices of 6 random chunks between generic filler. **6/6 were flagged**, with the planted chunk as BM25 top-1 every time. The planted excerpts scored c13 1.00 and LCS 149–591. A light paraphrase that dropped every fifth word still scored c13 0.64. The generic filler paragraph alone scored c13 0.087, which puts it in the review band. That matches the conclusion that the 0.08–0.14 band catches stock phrasing.

## Limits

- **Images are not covered.** 29 of 40 items are image-modality. Source packs that are only maps, photos, or scanned documents are not in `runner_input.jsonl` text, so they cannot be compared lexically. Text excerpts inside the sheet are covered, because the prompts carry the pdftotext output of the arkusz.
- **Short paragraphs are skipped.** Paragraphs under 100 normalized chars were not separate excerpt units, but they are still inside their prompt unit.
- **Lexical signals only.** Semantic paraphrase or translation of a source excerpt into Wikipedia prose would not be caught. BM25 top-5 is only a proxy for that.
- **The corpus snapshot is fixed.** The check covers index `350800b1…` only. A future `fetch --refresh` or new sources need a re-run, which is one command and takes seconds.
- **Keys were not compared.** Model answers and marking keys were not compared with the corpus. Answer facts are general historical knowledge and are allowed in retrieval by AGENTS.md.
- **Provisional thresholds.** They are heuristic and were set once. The positive control shows that verbatim and lightly edited passages clear them by a wide margin.
