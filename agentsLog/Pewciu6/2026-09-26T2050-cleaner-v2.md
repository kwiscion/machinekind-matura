## Changes (`scripts/Pewciu6/essay_contract.py`, revision `essay-contract-v2`)

1. **Headings.** `clean_body` no longer drops every Markdown-heading line. A heading-formatted line (`#…`, a bold-only
   line, a `Label:` line) is removed wholesale only when its text is a whitelisted structural label: Wstęp /
   Wprowadzenie / Rozwinięcie / Zakończenie / Podsumowanie / Wnioski / Teza / Argumentacja, `Aspekt [N] [–] <adjective>
   [i <adjective>]`, `WYPRACOWANIE`, or a restated topic header. A label containing a year or sentence punctuation is
   never structural. Any other heading that is a sentence (≥4 words, terminal punctuation) loses only its marker, and
   its text is **kept** (op `heading_marker_stripped`, 0 words removed). A short non-whitelisted heading that is not a
   sentence (a title, `## Aspekt polityczny 1343`) → `ambiguous_heading` → regenerate.
   Regression: the lead's `# W 1791 roku uchwalono Konstytucję trzeciego maja…` paragraph is kept verbatim.
2. **Text outside the JSON.** It must match a narrow non-substantive wrapper, line by line: a courtesy word, one
   announcing clause (`Oto wypracowanie w wymaganym formacie:`, ≤15 words, no second sentence), a code-fence marker, a
   word-count note, or a closing offer (`Mam nadzieję…`). A year outside the JSON is always content. Anything else →
   `ambiguous_text_outside_json`, whatever its length (the old ≤40-word allowance is gone). Regression: `Unia lubelska
   została zawarta w 1569 roku.` after valid JSON is rejected. The same rule covers extra JSON keys: a string value
   with >12 words or a year → `substantive_extra_key` (a `word_count: 480` field is still fine).
3. **Lexical checks are advisory.** `validate` reports `lexical_aspect_absent:*` and `lexical_source_marker_absent` in
   `validation.advisory`; they never gate, trigger repairs or discard prose. The hard mechanical gate is topic
   identity/ambiguity (topic_id, other-topic marks, topic headers), JSON/body shape and minimum length.

## Limits

The whitelist is hand-written. A structural label phrased differently is rejected, not removed; that costs a repair
or rewrite rather than losing prose. Advisory lexical flags are diagnostics only; aspect coverage is left to the
independent grader.
