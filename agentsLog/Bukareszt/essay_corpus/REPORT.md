# #117 grounded essay corpus: Greg's report (@Bukareszt)

Status: **in progress**. Counts in this file are regenerated by `bash scripts/Bukareszt/essay_corpus_build.sh`; the authoritative numbers are in `ledger.jsonl` and `export_v1/export_manifest.json`. Times are Europe/Warsaw, 2026-09-26. The work was CPU only: no training, GPU, HF upload, purchases or credential search.

## What this delivers
1. **Canonical source/topic groups.** `clusters.json` defines 68 clusters. The 207 sources (Greg's 107 pinned CC BY-SA/PD plus #4's 100) map to 149 canonical title keys, with aliases resolved and 0 unmapped. Root seed groups are mapped through `external_group_map`, and #80 DEV fixture ids through the #4 titles. The **cluster is the split unit**, and each cluster belongs to exactly one partition. Declared `dependencies` (root Sol: Augsburg↔Vienna, League↔Marshall) are merged with union-find, so a connected component can never span eval and train. `groups` writes `group_map.jsonl` and `leakage_report.json`: **0 fails**.
2. **Original eval16** (`eval16_topics.jsonl`): 16 items with two original topics each, all in clusters disjoint from every training source (Greg, root seed, root Sol, #4 strict) and from Paweł's #80 DEV. By era: antiquity 3, medieval 3, early modern 3, 19th c. 2, 20th c. 5. `eval16_factcards.jsonl` has 160 verbatim cards for grading reference only, never training. An independent card review is in `reviews/eval16_cards_review.json`. It flags 3 cards (ev-01-f03 omits a propaganda caveat; ev-04-f04 and ev-05-f09 overreach their quote) and marks eval-04 (Black Death) economic coverage as thin. No May 2023/2024/2025 exam material was opened; 2025 stays sealed. Validation-2024 source titles are compared only by canonical key, with 0 hits and nothing printed.
3. **Essays and repair pairs.** A generator subagent writes 400–500-word essays with verbatim fact cards from pinned revisions. The deterministic gate (`check`) enforces: body 400–500 words, ≥3 paragraphs, no preamble, closing, markdown or topic label; every quote verbatim in the pinned raw text with a matching source hash; source and card in the essay's own cluster; every number in the essay present in a used card; the cluster is a Greg training partition. A **different** reviewer subagent verifies facts and argument against the sources. `load_accepted` rejects self-review and verdicts whose `essay_sha256` doesn't match the text. One repair round is allowed, then the essay is dropped.
4. **Repair pairs** are deterministic transformations of *accepted* essays. There are three defect types: an invented assistant wrapper, an extra-topic paragraph from the same cluster (Temat labels plus a second answer), and underlength (intro plus conclusion only). The target is always the unchanged accepted essay. Each defect must fail the contract and each target must pass it. Provenance fields are `from_essay_id`, `essay_sha256`, `kind` and `variant`. An off-topic paragraph the reviewer flagged is never used, even as a defect.
5. **Export** (`export_v1/`): `train_sft.jsonl` holds chat messages (user exam prompt → assistant essay, and repair instruction+draft → clean essay) with no system prompt. `eval16_input.jsonl` holds runner rows `{id,prompt}`. Leakage is checked on components before writing.

## Reviewer findings that matter beyond this corpus
- **Pinned Wikipedia text is sometimes wrong**, so a source-grounded check is not the same as a correctness check. Examples: Verden massacre dated 799/800 (it was 782); Yalta credited with the specific western lands; Lithuania's independence placed at the August 1991 putsch; a "reparations paid in 2010" line that omits the Dawes/Young reductions. The independent reviewer caught all four.
- The most frequent draft defect was **drift into the other topic's material**, the same multi-topic failure #80 targets. The generator instruction now forbids it.
- **Cross-owner leakage warning:** root seed topics Casimir III, Lublin Union and January uprising/peasants share clusters with Paweł's #80 DEV fixtures. #4's 24 strict records touch 12 DEV clusters. An essay LoRA trained on those would topic-contaminate the #80 DEV comparison; eval16 is disjoint.

## Limitations
- There is one generator and one reviewer, and both are Claude Opus 5.5 subagents: independent contexts, not independent model families. The generator also used two helper agents to draft eval cards for 13 items, all re-verified by the generator and reviewed by the reviewer.
- Wikipedia is a tertiary source. The reviewer used the pinned text plus general knowledge to flag source errors, and no second source was fetched.
- Repair-pair diversity is limited to three template families with four wrapper variants.
- Root Sol's 8+8 records and their `additional_source_group_dependencies` field are not yet on `main`. When they land, `groups` will report any unmapped `source_group_id` until it is added to `external_group_map`.
