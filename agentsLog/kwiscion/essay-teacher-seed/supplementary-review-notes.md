1. **mhp-peasants**, Piotr Niziołek, Muzeum Historii Polski: [Uwłaszczenie chłopów w optyce Tymczasowego Rządu Narodowego](https://powstanie1863-64.pl/artykul/uwlaszczenie-chlopow-w-optyce-tymczasowego-rzadu-narodowego/index.htm). The article and footer declare CC BY, linked explicitly to [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Sections *Zmiany w Kongresówce*, *Wolni i równi*, *Najwyższa cena* support cards january-claim-01, 02 and 04: differing reform proposals, January 1863 ownership/compensation program, and substantial but not uniform peasant participation. The author emphasizes the importance of village support to sustaining the uprising. This counterbalances the Wikipedia article's stronger emphasis on inadequate mobilization. The target says participation existed but was not universal; it does not claim absence of support.

2. **radom-peasants**, Magdalena Grosiak, Muzeum Wsi Radomskiej, 3 February 2023: [Uwłaszczenie chłopów w Królestwie Polskim w dobie powstania styczniowego](https://muzeum-radom.pl/wydarzenia/uwlaszczenie-chlopow-w-krolestwie-polskim-w-dobie-powstania-styczniowego/3139). Read for reference only; the page reserves rights. Paragraphs on the Reds, January manifesto, uneven compliance, local peasant help and the 2 March 1864 ukaz corroborate cards january-claim-01, 02, 04 and 07. No verbatim text or image is included in the seed. The article says decrees entered into effect in early spring, then later broadly says the decree was not implemented because the uprising fell. Interpret the latter cautiously as incomplete realization, rather than erasing documented partial enforcement. The essay's wording about limited and uneven execution deliberately avoids that categorical inconsistency.
