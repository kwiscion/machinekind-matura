## Source grounding and functional quality

All 29 evidence cards match the claims embedded in the essays. I checked their historical content, the complete essays for additional claims, and whether evidence actually advances the requested argument. Dates, actors, causal links and qualifications are supported at the specificity used. Causal evaluations remain identifiable author synthesis rather than falsely attributed source verdicts. The essays use clean Polish prose, one topic each, six coherent paragraphs and 400–500 body words. They are useful modest exemplars; this review does not assign an official exam score.

- **Solon:** [Aristotle, parts 2, 5–9 and 12–14](https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/athenian_const.1.1.html) corroborates debt vulnerability, relief, political eligibility and subsequent conflict. The target qualifies traditional dating and late institutional testimony; it does not claim immediate mature democracy or abolition of all slavery.
- **Casimir:** the pinned biography supports accession, diplomacy, fortifications, regional statutes and eastern expansion. The [UJ archive history](https://archiwum.uj.edu.pl/historia-auj) corroborates the 1364 foundation and limited early operation. The target appropriately avoids a precise disputed statute date and an immediate fully functioning university claim. Diplomatic details could be richer, but the stated argument is functional and correct.
- **Lublin:** [AGAD’s archival description](https://agad.gov.pl/Unia%20Lubelska/uni_lub.html) corroborates the 1 July 1569 act and common institutions. The target distinguishes agreement from later royal signature by making no 4 July claim, preserves separate institutions and does not imply universal political equality. The incorporation account avoids falsely dating every incorporation to one day.
- **January Uprising:** [MHP’s Piotr Niziołek article](https://powstanie1863-64.pl/artykul/uwlaszczenie-chlopow-w-optyce-tymczasowego-rzadu-narodowego/index.htm) supports the program/compensation and substantial village participation; the [Radom museum article](https://muzeum-radom.pl/wydarzenia/uwlaszczenie-chlopow-w-krolestwie-polskim-w-dobie-powstania-styczniowego/3139) supports uneven enforcement, local support and the 1864 ukaz. Different source emphases do not invalidate the target’s cautious wording: real participation was not universal, and implementation was limited rather than wholly absent. The target does not claim the last resistance ended exactly in 1864.
