# Essay grading calibration for route selection

Evaluation-only instructions. Never include this document, grader examples, benchmark outputs or marking material in training or retrieval.

The independent DEV009–012 review gave35/60→39/60 while the owner gave14/60→27/60. That disagreement is large enough to change experiment selection. No existing scorecard is overwritten. The next paired essay wave must preserve annotated evidence, not just totals.

## Grading procedure

1. Freeze exact final-answer hashes and mechanically count submitted body words. Randomize anonymous answer labels; withhold arm, model and previous grades until the first pass is saved. Describe any accidental exposure honestly.
2. For each of the three required aspects, identify the actual supporting passage and list its relevant factual claims. Explain how the passage links evidence to the thesis. Classify the argument as absent, superficial, satisfactory or rich, scoring0/1/3/4. A date/name alone does not establish developed reasoning; a short causal explanation is not automatically superficial either. Do not require an unstated checklist of canonical events.
3. List distinct factual errors separately, with a source supporting the correction. Distinguish established falsehood from imprecise terminology, unsupported precision, disputed interpretation and omission. Apply the official factual-error deduction once to the narrative subtotal. A repeated false claim is not automatically several errors. Explain any effect on argument strength separately rather than silently doubling a penalty.
4. Grade coherence separately. Under300 body words gives0 for coherence, not automatically0 overall. For2 or1, identify the actual logical discontinuity, contradiction, irrelevant branch or unsupported conclusion. Repetition, headings or a factual error alone do not justify an automatic coherence deduction.
5. Retain failures and unsent answers at zero in the declared six-topic denominator. Score draft and final separately; do not substitute the draft for a failed final. Resolve factual correction disagreements before arguing about total points. A difference of more than2 total points, or a1-versus3 aspect disagreement, gets an independent adjudication. Preserve both initial grades and the final explanation.
