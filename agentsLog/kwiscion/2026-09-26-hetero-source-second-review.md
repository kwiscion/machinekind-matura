## Why the bounds matter

Central grading credits a correct required core when an extra false claim is separable from it. The low bound records a stricter examiner's possible rejection of that answer. This is not a blanket rule that wrong extra facts are harmless.

- **z1 final:** the chosen relief and required symbolic relation are correctly identified. The added explanation of the rejected image is false and unnecessary. Central 1, low 0; do not describe this as an undisputed clean answer.
- **z2 bare:** the required before/after comparison and political distinction are present, but invented bibliographic attribution, a false territory, and an unreliable date range damage the source account. Central 1, low 0. The final answer avoids these defects and is securely supported.
- **z4 bare:** both required features are clear. An odd extra adjective is treated as garbled wording rather than a second architectural thesis; no substantive score uncertainty is assigned.
- **z5.1:** bare's correct decision is insufficient because it identifies the wrong movement on the map. Final correctly distinguishes the two movements, with concrete source references. Its awkward language does not overturn that supported argument.
- **z14.1:** neither answer establishes the required relationship between the sources. The final answer substitutes geographic overlap and an invented coalition for identification of the actual mapped conflict. Zero in both, with no upper-bound credit.
- **z15.2:** both answers correctly identify the shared critical assessment. Bare transfers quoted language between sources and overstates a consequence. Final imports an unsupported characterization into the first source and misrepresents who expresses the criticism. These are source-fidelity errors, even though enough correct comparison survives for the central point. Both remain [0,1].

Bounds are examiner-judgment ranges, not statistical confidence intervals. The secure gain on z5.1 describes these exact answers only. Repeated matched trials or additional independent items are needed before attributing a stable benefit to the heterogeneous mechanism. Preserve the whole declared route; do not select individual lucky saved answers.
