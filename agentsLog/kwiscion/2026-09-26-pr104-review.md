## Findings

1. **P2 - A same-entity title match bypasses requested-relation relevance** (`scripts/Bukareszt/selective_rag.py:275`, also `303-311`). The OR condition passes on one distinctive title token even when the requested relation is absent from the chunk and selected window. Entirely invented repro: a 50-chunk synthetic index has title `Ormel`, body `Ormel mieszkał nad rzeką.` and 49 unrelated fillers; asking for Ormel's sailing teacher passes with `reason=title match`, one matched term and coverage 0.25. The body contains no teacher/sailing evidence. An independent pinned-index probe likewise accepts an article about a historical person for a fictitious monument-designer relation and includes an unrelated passage. Require entity plus requested-relation support in the actual compact passage, or abstain; retain no-answer/same-entity-wrong-relation fixtures. A source-level hit is insufficient.

2. **P2 - Explicit supplied-source extraction/explanation is routed as mixed** (`scripts/Bukareszt/selective_rag.py:242-245`). Generic verbs such as `podaj nazwe`, `wyjasnij`, and `dlaczego` count as external cues and override an explicit instruction to use the supplied text. Original synthetic prompt: a short text says Ormel sat under an oak, followed by a request to name the tree from that text. It routes `mixed` and, against the synthetic Ormel index above, inserts the irrelevant river passage. A source-only explanation prompt also routes `mixed`. Evidence need must be distinguished from answer form; generic verbs alone cannot prove external knowledge is required. Ambiguous cases should remain bare.

3. **P2 - Clear essays without one narrow keyword/point pattern bypass exclusion** (`scripts/Bukareszt/selective_rag.py:236-247`; `ESSAY_RE`). An original prompt beginning `Napisz rozprawkę` and explicitly requiring at least 300 words, a thesis, arguments and a conclusion is classified `external_fact`; the pinned-index gate passes and context is inserted. Add generic essay-form/minimum-length cues and default uncertain long-form requests to bare. Test multiple ordinary essay phrasings instead of only `wypracowanie` or 10+ point metadata.
